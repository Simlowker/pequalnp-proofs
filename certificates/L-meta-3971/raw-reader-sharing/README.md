# The unique reader of t cannot supply all binary sharing when its other input is primary

The 14-gate circuit sought in L-meta-3971 has nine raw bits, a free input x₁ OR t, at most one effective gate reading raw t, and a direct OR gate that reads t. Suppose that OR's other input is a primary input or a constant. The result here proves that **some other AND or OR gate must have its output used more than once** in the live computation. It permits arbitrary reuse of the unique inverter and of the required OR. The general task remains open.

Run `python3 verify_reader3971.py` in the extracted directory. It verifies the new result and its prerequisite integer certificate using standard-library Python.

Write the reader as t OR xⱼ. With all other binary gates used at most once, remove the reader and give its output freely to the remaining circuit. This costs one gate. The remaining inputs are x₀,…,x₇, x₁ OR t and t OR xⱼ. No other effective wire can use raw t. Forward reads yield a constant. If the OR is unary or duplicates t, its output is t and the earlier shared-inverter certificate suffices. If its companion is x₁, x₃, x₄, x₆, x₁ OR t, or the constant one, two inputs agree on all ten available signals but have opposite required outputs.

For j=0,2,5,7, all ten available signals can independently flip the required output from zero to one with the other nine fixed. The check finds each of these pairs on the original 512 inputs. If the inverter is live, write H for the monotone tree below it. Treat NOT H as one extra signal for the monotone outer tree. Let h and o be their leaf counts and f the number of outer occurrences of NOT H. The trees have h−1 and o−1 binary gates and the inverter costs one, so h+o≤14 after the reader is paid. The complement of H cannot increase on an isolated rise of any signal. Thus at least one positive occurrence of each of the ten signals is required outside the inverter. It follows that h+f≤4.

On positive and negative samples the verifier enumerates all inner columns of minimum cost k≤4−f. Each of the f occurrences of the complement contributes at most K to a signed rectangle partition; each other leaf contributes at most D. The matrices certify weight strictly above (14−k−f)D+fK in every case. If the inverter is unused, its gate still counts towards the task's fixed gate total and at most 12 binary gates remain after the reader and inverter are paid. A separate matrix for each basis rules out the resulting 13-leaf tree. Constants may be supplied freely throughout.
